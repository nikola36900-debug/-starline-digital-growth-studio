09 — GOOD QUESTIONS

Before we
get started.

Straight answers to the things business owners reasonably want to know.

How much does a business website cost?+

It depends on the number of pages, content needs, integrations, and complexity. After understanding your goals, we’ll provide a scoped quote before work begins.

How long does it take to build a website?+

Timelines depend on scope, feedback, and how quickly content and assets are ready. We’ll agree on milestones and a realistic timeline during planning.

Can you redesign my existing WordPress website?+

Yes. We can review the current site, identify usability and performance issues, and recommend a redesign or targeted improvements based on your needs.

Can you help my business appear on Google Maps?+

We can help with eligible Google Business Profile setup and optimisation, business information, and local SEO foundations. Visibility depends on several factors, and no placement can be guaranteed.

Do you guarantee first-page Google rankings?+

No. Search rankings are influenced by factors outside any agency’s control. We focus on sound SEO practices, relevant content, accurate business information, and measurable progress.

Will I own my website?+

Ownership, access, hosting, licences, and handover arrangements will be clearly described in your proposal so you know what you own and what third-party terms apply.

Can I update the website myself?+

WordPress websites can generally be updated by the owner. We can build with practical editing in mind and explain how to manage the parts you need to maintain.

How will we know if marketing is working?+

We’ll agree on relevant measures—such as search visibility, website actions, calls, and enquiries—then review the data in context. Tracking setup and available data will vary by project.

What do you need from me to get started?+

Start with a little information about your business, what you want to improve, and any existing website or business profile. We’ll use that to work out the next sensible step.
